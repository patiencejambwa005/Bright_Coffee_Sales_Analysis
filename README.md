# Bright_Coffee_Sales_Analysis
Analysis of Bright Coffee Shop Sales data to find revenue trends by time of day ,product and location using SQL  ,EXCEL  and a dashboard
This project analyses sales data from Bright Coffee Shop to understand how the business is performing and where revenue comes from 
The goal is to turn raw transaction data into insights and recommendations the business can act on such as when customers buy the most and which products sell best


##Approach

*Data Source*
*Imported the raw sales data from a CSV file of a coffee shop transactions

*ETL pipeline*
*Extracted the data, cleaned it handled missing values, fixed data types ,removed duplicates and loaded it into Databricks

*Data Storage*
*Organised the data in layers that is Bronze(raw),Silver(cleaned and validated) and Gold(ready for analysis and reporting)

*Data Processing*
*Used SQL in Databricks to answer key business questions including revenue by time of day , revenue by product , and revenue by store_location

*Data Visualisation*
*Built pivot tables and graphs in Excel and a dashboard in Power BI , Databricks , Looker Studio

*Business Output*
*Presented the findings and recommendations in a final presentation


##Key Insights
*Time of day , morning is the busiest period and brings in the most revenue followed by the afternoon and the evening and night sales are very low


##Recommendations
*Make sure there is enough staff and stock in the morning when demand is highest
*Consider promotions in the afternoon and evening to lift sales in the slower periods
